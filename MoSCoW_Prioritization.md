- Student registration and login
- Secure authentication
- Student dashboard
- Browse internships
- Apply for internships
- Project collaboration
- Student profile
- Faculty/Admin dashboard
- Internship approval

- Search internships
- Filters by location and domain
- Resume upload
- Notifications
- Team management

## Could Have
- Chat system
- Company ratings
- Calendar reminders
- Email notifications
- Certificate download

## Won't Have (Current Version)
- AI internship recommendations
- Video interviews
- Mobile application
- LinkedIn integration
- Real-time chat
